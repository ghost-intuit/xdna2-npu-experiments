YOLO26n vs YOLOv8n: work and data per block

Date: 16 October 2026, Version: 1.1, Last edit time: 15:38 AEDT, 17 October · pairs with the YOLO26n pages, version 1.1, and the YOLOv8n pages, version 1.7

A block-by-block comparison of YOLOv8n (ultralytics 8.2.103) and YOLO26n (ultralytics 8.4.178) at a 640×640 input. The YOLOv8n figures are read from the YOLOv8n pages [1], so both page sets give the same numbers. The YOLO26n figures are those of the YOLO26n pages [2]: the fused model with one set of three detection heads (P3, P4 and P5). The operations are defined on the two operations pages.

Bytes shown for: Applies to every byte figure on this page and is remembered on this page (stored separately from the YOLOv8n and YOLO26n page sets). int8: 8-bit integer; bf16: 16-bit brain floating point; fp16, fp32: 16-bit and 32-bit floating point; bfp16: block floating point. kB = 1,000 bytes, MB = 1,000,000 bytes. Weights are counted after batch normalisation is folded in: one weight per kernel entry plus one bias per output channel.*Note: for the bf16 and bfp16 definitions, see AMD Quark documentation: BF16 (bfloat16) and BFP16 (block floating point).

Work and data per block

Figure 1 – Work and data per block, YOLOv8n and YOLO26n

YOLOv8n

Totals: 4,371,456,000 multiplications, that is 8.74 billion operations counting 2 per multiply-add; Ultralytics publishes 8.7 GFLOPs [3].

Totals: Weights: 3,151,888 values; before batch normalisation is folded in, as trained, the model has 3,157,200 parameters, matching the published 3.2 million [3].

Totals: Activations in + out: 12,371,200 values, summed over the blocks in the chart; a tensor shared by two neighbouring blocks is counted once for each block.

YOLO26n

Totals: 2,740,153,600 multiplications (2,678,713,600 in the 102 convolutions + 61,440,000 in the 4 attention matrix products), that is 5.48 billion operations counting 2 per multiply-add; Ultralytics publishes 5.5 GFLOPs [4].

Totals: Weights: 2,408,932 values, matching the published 2.4 million [4]; before batch normalisation is folded in, as trained with both sets of detection heads, the model has 2,572,280 parameters [5].

Totals: Activations in + out: 14,939,200 values, summed over the blocks in the chart; a tensor shared by two neighbouring blocks is counted once for each block.

Counted over the blocks Stem to Head P5: the network on the main path, with one set of three detection heads (P3, P4 and P5). Decode, Top-300 selection and NMS are not included; see the post-processing table below. The three convolution rows add up to 63 convolutions in YOLOv8n and 102 in YOLO26n. YOLO26n also has 2 element-wise adds (the positional term inside each attention) and 2 scalings of q; they are not residual adds and are not listed. Each operation is counted once, whatever its size.

These steps are not drawn in Figure 1. They have no weights, and the operations pages give no activation figures for them, so they would have bars only in the Multiplications mode; their multiplications are at most 0.013 % of either model's total. NMS time depends on the number of boxes that pass the confidence threshold and is not shown by multiplications.

Ultralytics counts each leaf module (a module with no sub-modules, for example one convolution or one max-pool) as one layer, whatever its work (utils/torch_utils.py, lines 566–567). YOLO26n has more layers because it has more and smaller modules (102 convolutions against 63), and fewer multiplications mainly because its detection heads need 300.5 million instead of 1,810.6 million.

What changed

One line per block of YOLO26n that is new or changed compared with YOLOv8n, with the block page of each model. The texts are those of the YOLO26n diagram panels.

  • Stage P2 (Change from YOLOv8n): the C2f block is replaced by C3k2. The C3k2 of this layer splits at a quarter of its output width (e = 0.25: 16 of 64 channels), and each bottleneck then halves its channels in the middle (16 → 8, Bottleneck default e = 0.5). The output has 64 channels instead of 32.YOLOv8n: Stage P2 · YOLO26n: Stage P2
  • Stage P3 (Change from YOLOv8n): C2f with two bottlenecks is replaced by C3k2 with one bottleneck. B3 has 128 channels instead of 64.YOLOv8n: Stage P3 · YOLO26n: Stage P3
  • Stage P4 (Change from YOLOv8n): C2f with two bottlenecks is replaced by C3k2 with one C3k unit, which itself contains two 3×3 bottlenecks. The shape of B4 is unchanged.YOLOv8n: Stage P4 · YOLO26n: Stage P4
  • Stage P5 (Change from YOLOv8n): C2f with one bottleneck is replaced by C3k2 with one C3k unit. The output shape is unchanged.YOLOv8n: Stage P5 · YOLO26n: Stage P5
  • SPPF (Change from YOLOv8n): the output includes the residual add. In YOLOv8n, the SPPF output (S5) went directly to the neck; in YOLO26n, C2PSA comes between SPPF and the neck, and S5 is the C2PSA output.YOLOv8n: SPPF · YOLO26n: SPPF
  • C2PSA (New in YOLO26n): YOLOv8n has no attention.YOLOv8n: no such block · YOLO26n: C2PSA
  • 1 · N4 (Change from YOLOv8n): C2f is replaced by C3k2 with a C3k unit, whose bottlenecks have residual adds (the neck C2f blocks of YOLOv8n have none). The upsampled input S5 is now the C2PSA output, not the SPPF output.YOLOv8n: 1 · N4 · YOLO26n: 1 · N4
  • 2 · OUT3 (Change from YOLOv8n): C2f is replaced by C3k2 with a C3k unit. The concatenation has 256 channels instead of 192, because B3 is wider.YOLOv8n: 2 · OUT3 · YOLO26n: 2 · OUT3
  • 3 · OUT4 (Change from YOLOv8n): C2f is replaced by C3k2 with a C3k unit.YOLOv8n: 3 · OUT4 · YOLO26n: 3 · OUT4
  • 4 · OUT5 (Change from YOLOv8n): C2f is replaced by the attention form of C3k2, the second of the two attention blocks of YOLO26n (the first is C2PSA). The concatenated input S5 is now the C2PSA output, not the SPPF output.YOLOv8n: 4 · OUT5 · YOLO26n: 4 · OUT5
  • Inside each head (Change from YOLOv8n): there is no DFL (reg_max = 1): the box branch gives one value per edge distance instead of 16, so 4 values instead of 64, and it is 16 channels wide instead of 64. The class branch uses depthwise-separable convolutions instead of two full 3×3 convolutions. Each cell gives 84 values instead of 144.YOLOv8n: Inside each head · YOLO26n: Inside each head
  • Head P3 (Change from YOLOv8n): 84 values per position instead of 144. The detection head is trained with one-to-one assignment (one prediction per object), so its output does not need NMS.YOLOv8n: Head P3 · YOLO26n: Head P3
  • Head P4 (Change from YOLOv8n): 84 values per position instead of 144; one-to-one assignment.YOLOv8n: Head P4 · YOLO26n: Head P4
  • Head P5 (Change from YOLOv8n): 84 values per position instead of 144; one-to-one assignment.YOLOv8n: Head P5 · YOLO26n: Head P5
  • One-to-many heads (New as a drawn path): YOLOv8n has one set of detection heads, trained with one-to-many assignment. YOLO26 keeps such a set and adds the one-to-one set.YOLOv8n: Inside each head · YOLO26n: One-to-many heads
  • Decode + NMS (Change from YOLOv8n): the same steps, without the DFL step, on 84 values per position instead of 144. In YOLO26n this step belongs only to the secondary path.YOLOv8n: Decode + NMS · YOLO26n: Decode + NMS
  • Decode (Change from YOLOv8n): there is no DFL step (YOLOv8n: softmax over 16 bins and an expected value for each edge distance). The boxes come out as corner coordinates x1, y1, x2, y2.YOLOv8n: Decode + NMS · YOLO26n: Decode
  • Top-300 selection (New in YOLO26n): replaces NMS on the main path.YOLOv8n: Decode + NMS · YOLO26n: Top-300 selection

Abbreviations

YOLO
You Only Look Once: the whole image is processed in a single pass of the network.
P2, P3, P4, P5
Pyramid level n: the feature map shrunk by 2ⁿ (P3 = 8×, P4 = 16×, P5 = 32×).
Up / Down
Upsample ×2 (nearest neighbour) / downsample ×2 (stride-2 convolution).
SiLU
Sigmoid Linear Unit: the activation function, x · sigmoid(x).
Sigmoid
The function 1 / (1 + e⁻ˣ); maps any value to the range 0 to 1.
CSP
Cross Stage Partial: split channels so only part goes through the heavy layers, then merge.
C2f
CSP bottleneck with 2 convolutions: YOLOv8's main feature block.
C3k2
Ultralytics block, a form of C2f whose inner units can be C3k blocks (or a bottleneck followed by a PSABlock). YOLO26's main feature block.
C3k
Ultralytics block: a C3 block (CSP bottleneck with 3 convolutions) with a selectable kernel size. Used as the inner unit of C3k2.
PSABlock
Attention followed by an FFN, each with a residual add.
C2PSA
CSP block with PSABlocks on half of the channels (C2f-style split, 2 convolutions).
Attention
An operation in which each position takes a weighted sum over all positions; the weights come from the products of queries and keys, normalised by softmax.
q, k, v
Query, key and value: the three tensors that an attention block computes from its input.
FFN
Feed-forward network: here two 1×1 convolutions, the first with SiLU.
Softmax
Turns a row of values into positive values that sum to 1: eˣ divided by the sum of eˣ over the row.
SPPF
Spatial Pyramid Pooling – Fast: chained max-pools that add context at several sizes.
One-to-one
Training assignment in which each object is matched to exactly one prediction. The detection heads learn to give one prediction per object, so no NMS is needed.
One-to-many
Training assignment in which each object is matched to several predictions, as in YOLOv8. The output contains duplicates and needs NMS.
reg_max
Number of bins per box-edge distance. YOLOv8: 16 (with DFL). YOLO26: 1 (one value per distance, no DFL).
DFL
Distribution Focal Loss: in YOLOv8, each box-edge distance is predicted as probabilities over 16 bins. Not used in YOLO26.
NMS
Non-Maximum Suppression: removes overlapping duplicate boxes, keeping the best.
x1, y1, x2, y2
Box corners: left, top, right, bottom, in pixels.
FLOPs, GFLOPs
Floating-point operations; GFLOPs: billions of them. Counted as 2 per multiply-add.
int8, bf16, fp16, fp32, bfp16
Number formats: 8-bit integer; 16-bit brain floating point; 16-bit and 32-bit floating point; block floating point (on the operations and block pages).
kB, MB
Kilobyte (1,000 bytes), megabyte (1,000,000 bytes).
AEDT
Australian Eastern Daylight Time, the time zone of the edit times.
