Runs on the CPU. In the Ultralytics model the softmax, expectation and sigmoid sit inside the Detect layer; in this diagram they belong to Decode. Exponentials and divisions are not counted as multiplications. NMS work depends on how many boxes pass the threshold, so no count is given.
