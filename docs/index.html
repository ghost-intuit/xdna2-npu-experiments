xdna2-npu-experiments

Neural network inference on the AMD XDNA 2 NPU

Hello, and welcome.

This site collects experiments running neural network inference on the AMD XDNA 2 NPU in Ryzen AI laptops, using the IRON / MLIR-AIE toolchain and AMD's Vitis AI flow.

The work is in progress. Results and write-ups will be added here as they are ready to share.

Contents

  • Getting the TOPS: AMD XDNA 2 NPU on a Windows laptop, Vitis AI vs IRON (PDF). An experimental report comparing AMD's Vitis AI path with hand-written designs on the open IRON toolchain, on simple synthetic convolution networks.
  • YOLOv8 architecture and per-block operations (operations in detail). An interactive diagram of the YOLOv8 object detector, from input image through backbone, neck and detection heads to the final boxes, with the mathematical operations, tensor shapes and multiplication counts of each block for YOLOv8n at 640×640.
